# CheckSteady — Privacy Notice

**Version 2026-09-03.** For people who *use* CheckSteady: staff at customer
sites, and people who contact us about it.

**If you are a resident** whose presence is recorded in CheckSteady, this notice
is not the one you want. The organisation running your accommodation decides
what is recorded about you and why, and it must give you its own privacy notice.
We only hold that data on their behalf. Ask them, or write to us at
aimee@tenzing.ie and we will pass your request to them.

---

## Who we are

Tenzing Digital, Ireland, is the controller of the personal data described
below. Contact: **aimee@tenzing.ie**.

## What we hold, and why

**Your staff account** — name, email address, role, whether the account is
active. We hold this to give you access to the service and to attribute records
to the person who made them, which is the point of an audit trail. Lawful basis:
performance of the contract with your employer, and our legitimate interest in a
service whose records can be relied on.

**Login and session records** — when you signed in, from which IP address, and
which browser reported itself. We hold this to keep accounts secure and to
investigate suspected misuse. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, namely
security. Sessions expire after 12 hours; login records are kept for 90 days.

**Correspondence** — if you email us, we keep the exchange so we can help you
and so we have a record of what was agreed. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
Kept for two years after our last contact.

**Signup and billing records** — the organisation, who accepted our terms, when,
and which version. Lawful basis: legal obligation and performance of the
contract. Kept for six years, as Irish tax and company law requires.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it
to train machine learning models.

## Who else sees it

Our sub-processors, and nobody else, except where the law requires disclosure:

- **Render Services, Inc.** — hosting and database. Data stored in Frankfurt.
- **Resend, Inc.** — sending account emails such as password resets.

Where any transfer outside the EEA arises, it is made under an adequacy decision
or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected, to have
it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a
portable format. Where we rely on legitimate interests you may object, and we
will stop unless we have compelling grounds not to.

To exercise any of these, email **aimee@tenzing.ie**. We will respond within one
month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you may complain to the
**Data Protection Commission**, 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 X963 —
[dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie).

## Cookies

The application sets **one** cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in.
It is essential to the service, contains no tracking identifier, cannot be read
by JavaScript, and is deleted when you sign out or when the session expires.

We use no analytics, no advertising cookies, and no third-party trackers. The
marketing website loads no fonts, scripts or images from anyone else, which is
why it has no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

## Changes

If we change this notice we will publish the new version with a new version date
and, where the change is significant, tell account holders by email.
