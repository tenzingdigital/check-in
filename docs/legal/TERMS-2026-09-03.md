## 1. What CheckSteady is

CheckSteady is a daily welfare register and door log for residential
accommodation. It records that a resident presented at your site on a given
day, who recorded it, and when; it produces lists of who has not been seen and
reports against thresholds you configure.

**It is a record-keeping tool, not a decision-maker.** It reports counts and
states the rules you have configured. It does not decide that a person has
breached anything, does not classify anyone, and never contacts a resident, a
regulator or any third party on your behalf. Every decision arising from what
it reports — a warning letter, a report to IPAS, a referral, a change to
someone's accommodation — is yours, made by a person, on your judgement.
