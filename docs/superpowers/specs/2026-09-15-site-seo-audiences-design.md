# Search reach for IPAS and other residential centres — design

Written 15 September 2026 from the owner's ask: "enhance the SEO for IPAS
centres and any other residential centres". Decisions taken while writing
it: the brochure site already does the mechanics (titles, canonicals,
Open Graph, schema.org FAQ and breadcrumbs, a sitemap, one IPAS page), so
this is about the words people search and the audiences the site speaks
to, not about tags; every new audience is written as **built for**, never
**used by** — the owner serves one IPAS centre and no one else yet, and
the site must not pretend otherwise; and the sensitive sectors (refuges,
treatment, HIQA care) get pages because the owner is willing to adapt the
product to them, with the page saying what it is not.

## What is wrong now

- The home page's `<title>`, description and Open Graph text never say
  "IPAS" or "direct provision" — the two terms the buyer types. They say
  "residential sites … hostels, supported housing, student residences and
  care settings".
- `/for/ipas-accommodation/` is written for someone who already knows the
  jargon. It never uses "direct provision", "International Protection
  Accommodation Services", "IPAS inspection", "daily register", "weekly
  register" or "permitted absence", and it predates the Weekly Register
  Update, the overnight safeguarding alert and the Excel exports.
- There are three audience pages (IPAS, homeless hostels, student
  residences). Everyone else who keeps a 24-hour register or a visitor
  book lands on the generic home page or nowhere.

## Scope

1. **Home page** (`site/index.html`, hand-written) — **quiet front door.**
   The owner's call on 15 September, after weighing the attention a
   "software for direct provision" front page draws from people who are
   not buyers: the `<title>`, description and Open Graph text stay
   sector-neutral — "accommodation and residential centres", the
   audiences by their plain names — and do **not** say IPAS or direct
   provision. Those terms live on the audience pages, which are what a
   centre manager's search lands on. The `SoftwareApplication` schema
   gains an `audience` list in the same neutral wording; the "Who it's
   for" section gains a card per new page. The hero copy stays as it is.

2. **`/for/ipas-accommodation/`** rewritten for the searched terms without
   dumbing it down, and brought current with the features shipped since
   9 September (weekly register update, safeguarding alert, Excel and
   inspection pack, unsubscribe is not marketing).
3. **Five new audience pages**, generated by `tools/build-site.py` with the
   same `page()`/`sec()`/`faq_html()` template as the existing three:

   | Path | Buyer | Searched terms to carry |
   |---|---|---|
   | `/for/emergency-and-supported-accommodation/` | Section 10 emergency accommodation, family hubs, AHB supported and sheltered housing | "emergency accommodation register", "family hub", "supported housing sign-in", "approved housing body" |
   | `/for/ukrainian-and-botp-accommodation/` | Temporary-protection centres for beneficiaries of temporary protection | "Ukrainian accommodation centre", "BOTP", "temporary protection accommodation", "Department of Children, Equality, Disability, Integration and Youth" |
   | `/for/nursing-homes-and-residential-care/` | HIQA designated centres: nursing homes, disability residential | "HIQA fire register", "nursing home visitor book", "fire evacuation list", "residents register HIQA" — and an explicit "not a care-record or medication system" |
   | `/for/refuges-and-treatment-centres/` | Domestic-violence refuges, residential addiction treatment | "refuge visitor log", "who is on site", "residential treatment attendance", "authorised leave" — and an explicit paragraph on confidentiality: no addresses stored, counts-only email, EU hosting, erasure |
   | `/for/visitor-and-contractor-sign-in/` | Offices, factories, schools, community buildings wanting a sign-in book and fire roll call | "visitor sign-in app", "contractor sign in sheet", "fire roll call app", "visitor book replacement" |

   Each page: unique title and description; eyebrow + H1 + lede; two or
   three sections in the house voice; four or five FAQ items in both the
   visible FAQ and the `FAQPage` schema (check-site enforces the match);
   breadcrumb schema; the shared CTA; three "Read next" links; a sitemap
   entry; a card on the home page; a footer "Who it's for" link.
4. **Housekeeping:** sitemap `lastmod` bumped on every changed page;
   footer nav lists every `/for/` page; `tools/check-site.py` gains two
   checks — every page's title and description are unique, and no page
   under `site/` claims customers it does not have (a forbidden-phrase
   list: "used by", "trusted by", "our customers", "customers include",
   "already use", "centres use").

Out of scope: blog posts, backlinks, keyword pages, paid search, any
change to the app, any claim the product does not back.

## Voice and truth rules

- **No centre is ever named or located** anywhere on the site — no case
  study, no logo wall, no "trusted by", no town. The one IPAS customer
  stays invisible. `tools/check-site.py` forbids the customer phrases
  below; the reviewer reads for a name or a place.
- **Owner item, outside this change:** a contact address that is not a
  person's name (e.g. `hello@checksteady.com`, forwarded) so any
  hostility lands on a mailbox. Until it exists the site keeps the
  current address; when it does, one search-and-replace in
  `tools/build-site.py` and `site/index.html`.

- Every audience page says what the product **is** for that buyer and
  what it **is not**. The HIQA page names the fire register, visitor book
  and roll call and says in its second paragraph that it is not a care
  plan, a medication record or a nursing record. The refuge page says
  addresses are never stored, that the Sunday email carries counts only,
  and that a site can be erased on request.
- "Built for", "designed for", "suited to". Never "used by", "trusted by",
  "our customers". The one true claim — built with an accommodation centre
  manager's feedback — stays on the IPAS page where it is true.
- Search terms appear in headings, the lede and the FAQ questions, once
  each where they read naturally. No lists of synonyms, no hidden text.
- Ireland first (IPAS, HIQA, Tusla, Section 10 are Irish); the UK is
  mentioned where the existing copy already does.

## Testing

`python3 tools/check-site.py` after `python3 tools/build-site.py` — every
FAQ question visible, every JSON-LD block parses, canonical + OG + title
on every page, every internal link resolves, every page in the sitemap,
titles and descriptions unique, no forbidden customer phrases. `check.sh`
already runs it. A reviewer reads every new page for a claim the product
does not back.
