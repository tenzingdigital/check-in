How CheckSteady works

Written so that a new staff member can start on a shift with no training. Each part is a task, in the order you meet it. Two things to know before anything else: the Door records people passing in and out, and the Daily register records the once-a-day check-in. They are different acts, and the app keeps them apart on purpose.

Start here

Logging in

Open the site on any phone, tablet or computer and enter your email and password. If you have never had a password, use Forgot your password? and a link arrives by email; it works once and for a day.

Supervisors and administrators may be asked for a six-digit code from their email after the password, if the site requires it or the login looks unusual. Type the code; tick Trust this device only on your own phone, never on a shared terminal.

Which screen am I on?

The first time you log in on a device you are asked What are you recording? Pick the job for this terminal. You can switch at any time with the pill at the top of the screen: blue is the door, green is the daily register. The colour follows you onto the buttons and the swipe strips, so you always know which record you are writing.

Who can do what

Guard Records at the door and on the register, runs a roll call, reads the log. Sees names, ages and states, never a date of birth.
Supervisor Everything a guard does, plus adding and editing residents, importing a spreadsheet, buildings and rooms, and exporting reports.
Admin Everything a supervisor does, plus staff accounts, the site's settings and feature switches, a resident's full record export and erasure, and the log of who viewed which record.

A refusal always says so in words. If a button does nothing for you, it is not yours to press, and the person who can is named in the message. The full table is under Who can do what.

The screen explains itself

Hold a tile or a tab for a sentence about what it counts. A round ? beside a label opens the same kind of note. The first time you open each screen on a device, a short card at the top says the three things that matter; tap Got it and it never returns.

The door every staff member

The door answers one question: who is on site right now? Every sign-in and sign-out is a movement with a time and your name on it.

Sign someone in or out

1Find the person. The whole list is on screen; type part of a name, or a room number such as B1, to filter. Spelling, accents and name order are forgiven.
2Look at the person. The app records that you checked; it does not check for you.
3Swipe the card right to sign IN, left to sign OUT. The strip behind the card says which. Or tap the name and use the Sign IN and Sign OUT buttons.

The card updates the moment the movement is recorded. Nothing can be edited afterwards: a mistake is corrected by recording the opposite movement, and both stay on the log.

The three tiles

On site, Off site and Moved today count the list below them. Tap a tile to see only those residents; tap it again for everyone. The line under the search box says which filter is on.

Several people at once

A family coming back from school, a minibus arriving: tap Select several, tick each person, then Sign IN or Sign OUT in the bar at the bottom. Ticking someone who is in a family ticks the whole family that is on screen. Each person still gets their own movement with the time and your name. Cancel or Stop selecting puts the list back to normal.

Since when

Every card says on site since 14:20 or off site since Thu 4 Sep 09:10: the time of the person's last movement, with the day once it is not today. No movement on record means they have never been signed in.

The log

The Log tab lists every movement, newest first, with who recorded it. It opens on today; tap Yesterday, This week or This month, or set the two dates yourself for up to a month at a time, and type part of a name or a room in the box to see only those movements.

One person's history

Tap a name and scroll under the buttons: History lists every movement and check-in for that person over the last 30 days, with the date, the time and who recorded it. Tap a quick range or change the two dates for any range up to a year. The same panel is on the register sheet and on the admin edit sheet.

The daily register every staff member

The register answers a different question: has this resident presented today? The rule is one check-in per calendar day, midnight to midnight in the site's time zone. Being signed in at the door does not count unless the site has turned on "Door sign-in counts as check-in" under Settings; then a sign IN (never a sign OUT) is recorded as the day's check-in, marked as from the door.

Record a check-in

1The list opens on Not seen: residents required today who have not checked in yet. Type a name to find anyone, seen or not.
2Look at the person, then swipe the card either way, or tap the name and press Check in.
3The card drops off the Not seen list and the tile counts change. A second check-in on the same day is recorded but changes nothing.

The tiles

Not seen is today's work: residents required today who have not checked in. Seen today is who has already checked in, and each card says the time. Runs of missed nights and days absent in the rolling window are not on this screen; supervisors and admins read them under Admin → Absences.

Away with the centre's agreement

A resident on an authorised absence (a holiday, a family matter, hospital) shows Away until a date on their card, on the Door and the register, and is not counted as not seen. The daily rule does not apply on those days, so they never count as missed. A supervisor records the absence on the person's edit sheet under Admin.

Under 18s

Residents under the site's adult age are listed but not required. Their cards say so, and they are never counted as not seen.

The detail sheet

Tap a name for the sheet: the identity document type and number, the time of today's check-in and every check-in recorded today with the name of whoever recorded it, consecutive missed nights and days absent in the rolling window each shown beside the figure in Settings ("2 of 3", "9 of 10"), the last 30 days as a strip (green seen, red missed, grey not required; tap a cell for the date and, for a green cell, the time), and a note when a count has reached a figure. The note states the count and the figure; it never gives a verdict. Supervisors and admins can add or change the ID number here.

The ID number is shown on the sheet only, never in a list, and every opening of the sheet is logged with your name. That is by design: an inspector can ask who looked at a record.

Roll call and evacuation every staff member

On the Door, the Roll call tab lists everyone recorded on site, by building, with people who need assistance first. It appears when the site has the evacuation feature on. During a roll call, swipe a row either way, or tap Safe; a swipe never un-marks anyone.

Run a drill or a real roll call

1Tap Start a drill or Roll call now. Every other warden's phone shows the same roll call within a few seconds.
2At the assembly point, tap Safe beside each person you can see. A tick made on one phone appears on the others; a person is counted once however many wardens tick them.
3The amber line says how many people needing assistance are still to find. Tap it to show only them; tap again for everyone.
4Tap End at the top of the list. If people are still unaccounted for the app asks you to confirm. The drill is kept as a record with who was accounted for, by whom, and when. A drill and a real roll call work the same way; the header says which one is running, and the record keeps the difference.

Print the evacuation list gives a paper copy per building for the fire panel, with the assistance needs shown.

With no connection, each phone keeps its own ticks and they merge when the network returns. Two phones cannot see each other's ticks while both are offline.

Visitors, staff and contractors every staff member

The Visitors tab on the Door is for anyone on site who is not a resident: staff, visitors, contractors and suppliers. It appears when the site has the visitors feature on. A fire officer asks who is in the building, not who lives there; this is the other half of that answer.

1Pick who they are: Visitor, Contractor, Supplier or Staff. Type the name and, if useful, the company or reason. Tap Sign in.
2They appear under On site with the time and your name. When they leave, tap Sign out beside them; they move to Left today.
3During a roll call, everyone still signed in is listed under Staff, visitors and contractors and is marked safe like a resident. The count at the top includes them.

Only a kind, a name, an optional company and the times are held, and the record is kept as long as the movement log. Signing visitors in and out needs a connection; the roll call keeps the last on-site list on the phone so a drill can still count them.

Admin supervisors and admins

Residents

Add takes a first name, last name and date of birth, and optionally the ID document, a room and an evacuation need. The date of birth is used only for the adult rule and is never shown to guards.

Tap a resident for the edit sheet: change details, move rooms, set an evacuation need, link family members, mark them departed when they leave (the record stays for the retention period), or as an admin export or erase the whole record.

Authorised absences

On a resident's edit sheet: the first and last day away and a reason from the list (holiday, family, medical, education, work, other). For a child, tick that a parent or guardian agreed; the app refuses the absence without it. If the person comes back early, Back early: end today ends it; a planned absence that never happened is cancelled. Nothing else is held: no destination, no notes. The register shows those days as not required.

Rooms so far

The same sheet lists every room the person has had, with the dates and who moved them. It fills in by itself as rooms change and closes when someone leaves; nothing to maintain.

Make a family

Where Households is on: tap Make a family above the list, tick each person who lives as one family, then Make a family in the bar at the bottom. If one of them is already in a family, the others join it. A family stays together on the roll call, children marked, and a room reads as a family. On any edit sheet the family's members are chips; tap one to open that person, or add someone from the drop-down.

Import a spreadsheet

1Save your list as a CSV file with headings: First name, Last name, Date of birth, and optionally ID type, ID number, Building, Floor, Room, Evacuation need. Download the template.
2Import, choose the file. Every line gets a verdict: ready, already on the register, or the problem to fix, such as a date that does not exist or a room the site does not have.
3Tap Import N residents. Only the ready lines are added. Fix the sheet and import it again: anyone already here is skipped, never duplicated.

Dates can be written day first (14/03/1988) or year first (1988-03-14). The evacuation column can be words, such as "needs help to move" or "wheelchair".

Absences

Every active resident with a run of consecutive missed nights or a missed day in the rolling window, worst first, each count beside the figure in Settings. A count that has reached its figure is marked. Nothing is decided here; the letter is the manager's.

Buildings and rooms

Describe the site once: buildings, floors and room numbers as painted on the doors, with a capacity. Assign residents to rooms on their edit sheet. The tab then shows occupancy per room: each person is a chip with a filled dot for on site, a hollow dot for off site, child where they are under the adult age and their evacuation need where that feature is on. Tap a chip to open their record. That is the list a fire officer asks for.

Reports

The daily register, attendance, the movement log, Out and back, the drill record, Roll call: who was marked safe, visitors, Authorised absences, Room history and Absent overnight for a date range; occupancy, the evacuation list and Absent now as they stand. Each can be viewed and printed, or downloaded as a spreadsheet. A reason is required and every export is logged with it. Administrators also have Who viewed which record.

Out and back is the check-out and check-in list: one row per absence with the building, room, date and time out, date and time back, hours away, and who signed each. A row with the in-columns blank is someone still out. Absent now is everyone off site at this moment, with when they left and who signed them out. Absent overnight is who was off site at midnight on each night in the range: the app takes that snapshot itself just after midnight, site time, and keeps it as long as the register, so the answer for a night months ago is still there after the movement log has been cleared.

Staff admins

Invite a staff member with their email and role. They receive a link to choose their own password; nobody is ever told a password. Disable an account when someone leaves: their sessions end at once. Roles can be changed at any time; you cannot demote or disable yourself.

Settings admins

  • Register rules: the adult age, the hour after which "not seen" turns amber, and the house rules for consecutive missed nights and days absent in a window.
  • Features for this site: buildings, evacuation, households, visitors and "Door sign-in counts as check-in" are off until turned on here. Turning one off hides its screens; nothing recorded is deleted.
  • Retention: how long movements and the register are kept. The IPAS policy says six months for the register; the audit trail and access log follow it.
  • Security: the idle lock, whether supervisors and admins need an emailed code, and the home countries a login is expected from.

Working offline

The door and the register keep working when the connection drops. The pill in the header changes from Online to Offline; the list you last saw stays on screen, and anything you record is queued and marked Queued on the card.

When the connection returns the queue is sent in order, with the time you actually recorded it, and the cards update. A queued event survives a logout and is sent the next time anyone logs in on that device. Events older than the site's sync window are refused and listed so you can re-record them.

The copy of the register held on the device is encrypted. Logging out does not delete queued events, on purpose; locking the screen after idle minutes does not either.

When something goes wrong

I am locked out after wrong passwords

Five minutes, then try again. Use Forgot your password? if you are unsure of it.

The screen locked itself

The idle lock: after the site's idle minutes with no activity the screen returns to the login. Nothing was lost; log in again.

The code by email did not arrive

Codes last ten minutes. Check the spam folder, then start again from the login screen for a new one. If the site cannot send email at all, the login is refused with the security contact address in the message: tell your administrator.

"Only an administrator can…" or "Not authorised"

The action is for a higher role. Ask a supervisor or admin; the message names which.

My login was refused because of where I am

Supervisors and admins can only log in from the site's home countries. If you are travelling, ask an admin to add the country under Settings, or use a guard account.

A resident is missing from the list

They may be marked departed: an admin can see them under Residents with the Departed filter and reactivate them. Or they were never added: Add or Import.

Something says Queued and stays that way

The device has no connection. Check the pill in the header. The event is safe and will be sent when the connection returns.

The nightly job is late

A banner appears on the register when the close-out has not run by two in the morning, site time. Until it runs, yesterday is not yet on the record as missed, and Admin → Absences may be a day behind. Tell your administrator; it is a hosting matter, not a data one.

Privacy on one page

  • Held about a resident: name, date of birth, ID document type and number, room, evacuation need, household, and every movement, check-in and register day.
  • Who sees what: guards see names, ages and states; the date of birth is for supervisors and admins; the ID number is on the detail sheet only; the evacuation need is on the roll call and evacuation list only.
  • Kept for: movements 90 days, the register six months, both set by the site. The audit trail and the access log follow the register.
  • On the record: every change an administrator makes, every export and its reason, every opening of a resident's record, and every login attempt.
  • A resident's rights: an admin can export everything held about a person as one file, or erase them entirely, leaving only a proof that an erasure happened.
  • Where: one provider, in Frankfurt, in the European Union. Nothing is sent to a third party.

The full statement is in the site's privacy notice and data processing agreement.
