How CheckSteady works

Two records, kept apart on purpose: In & out is who passed the door; the Daily register is who checked in today. Pick what you want to do.

  • Sign someone in or out
  • Record today's check-in
  • See who is off site
  • Run a drill or evacuation
  • Sign a visitor or staff in
  • Add or change a resident
  • Record an authorised absence
  • Print or export a report
  • Set up a tablet
  • Something is wrong

Start here

Log in, choose the screen for this device, and it opens there every time.

  1. Enter your email and password.No password yet? Forgot your password? emails a link that works once, for a day.
  2. Answer What are you recording?In & out for the gate tablet, Daily register for the check-in tablet.
  3. Tick Always open this on this tablet.That device skips the question from then on. ?view=choose on the address undoes it.
  • Blue is In & out
  • Green is the register
  • Hold a tile for what it counts
  • ? beside a label explains it
More detail

Supervisors and administrators may be asked for a six-digit code from their email after the password, if the site requires it or the login looks unusual. Type the code; tick Trust this device only on your own phone, never on a shared terminal.

You can switch screens at any time with the pill at the top. The colour follows you onto the buttons and the swipe strips, so you always know which record you are writing. Opening the app with ?view=inout or ?view=register on the address fixes a device's job the same way as the tick.

The first time you open each screen on a device, a short card at the top says the three things that matter; tap Got it and it never returns.

Who can do what

Guard Sign in and out, check-ins, roll call, the log. Names and ages, never a date of birth.
Supervisor All of that, plus residents, absences, buildings and rooms, the staff list, reports.
Admin All of that, plus staff accounts, settings, exports and erasures, and who viewed what.

A refusal always says so in words, and names who can.

More detail

The three tiles. On site, Off site and Moved today count the list below them. Tap a tile to see only those residents; tap again for everyone.

Since when. Every card says on site since 14:20 or off site since Thu 4 Sep 09:10, the time of the last movement, with the day once it is not today. No movement on record means never signed in.

Nothing is edited afterwards. A wrong movement is corrected by recording the opposite one, and both stay on the log with the times.

Select several. A family coming back from school, a minibus arriving: tap Select several, tick each person, then Sign IN or Sign OUT in the bar at the bottom. Each person still gets their own movement with the time and your name. ✕ or Stop selecting puts the list back.

Spelling. Accents and name order are forgiven; a near miss still finds the person.

See who is off site, today or any day

Three places, depending on the question.

  1. Right now: tap the Off site tile on In & out.Everyone out, with since when.
  2. One person: tap the name, scroll to History.Every movement and check-in with date, time and who recorded it. Any range up to a year.
  3. A period: the Log tab.Yesterday, This week, This month or two dates; type a name or room to filter.
For a report or an inspection

Supervisors and admins have three reports under Admin → Reports. Absent now is everyone off site this moment, with when they left, who signed them out, and whether the absence is authorised. Absent overnight is who was off site at midnight on each night in a range: the app takes that snapshot itself just after midnight and keeps it as long as the register, so a night months ago is still there after the movement log has been cleared. Out and back is the check-out and check-in list: one row per absence with room, date and time out, date and time back, hours away, and who signed each. All three mark under-18s, so a child's overnight absence stands out.

Record today's check-in every staff member

Has this resident presented today? One check-in per day, midnight to midnight. Being signed in at the door is a different thing.

  1. The list opens on Not seen.Residents required today who have not checked in. Type a name to find anyone.
  2. Look at them, then swipe the card either way.Or tap the name and press Check in.
  3. The card drops off the list.A second check-in the same day changes nothing.
  • Under 18: listed, not required
  • Away until: an authorised absence, not counted
  • Missed nights are under Admin → Absences
More detail

The tiles. Not seen is today's work. Seen today is who has already checked in, and each card says the time. Runs of missed nights and days absent in the rolling window are not on this screen; supervisors and admins read them under Admin → Absences.

The detail sheet. Tap a name: the ID document type and number, today's check-ins with who recorded each, consecutive missed nights and days absent in the window each shown beside the figure in Settings ("2 of 3", "9 of 10"), the last 30 days as a strip (green seen, red missed, grey not required; tap a cell for the date and time), and a note when a count has reached a figure. The note states the count; it never gives a verdict. Supervisors and admins can add or change the ID number here.

Away with the centre's agreement. A resident on an authorised absence shows Away until a date on their card and is not counted as not seen; those days never count as missed.

The door switch. Where the site has turned on "An In & out sign-in counts as check-in" under Settings, a sign IN (never a sign OUT) is also recorded as the day's check-in. Leave it off where the policy makes residents responsible for their own check-in.

The ID number is on the sheet only, never in a list, and every opening of the sheet is logged with your name. An inspector can ask who looked at a record.

Run a drill or a real evacuation every staff member

The Roll call tab on In & out: everyone on site by building, people who need assistance first. Every warden's phone at once.

  1. Tap Practice drill or Real evacuation.The record keeps the two apart. Other wardens' phones show it within seconds.
  2. At the assembly point, mark each person Safe.Tap or swipe. Tap a building to see only your block; type a name or room to find one person.
  3. Watch the amber line: how many needing assistance are still to find.Tap it to show only them.
  4. Tap End, add one line for the record.Which alarm, the assembly point, what went wrong. Never about a person.
  • Staff and visitors are on the list too
  • A tick on one phone shows on the others
  • Print the evacuation list for the fire panel
More detail

A person is counted once however many wardens tick them; a swipe never un-marks anyone. The drill is kept as a record with who was accounted for, by whom, when, and the note. The report Roll call: who was marked safe lists it person by person.

With no connection each phone keeps its own ticks and they merge when the network returns. Two phones cannot see each other's ticks while both are offline.

Sign a visitor, contractor or staff member in every staff member

The Visitors tab on In & out: anyone on site who is not a resident. A fire officer asks who is in the building, not who lives there.

  1. Pick Visitor, Contractor, Supplier or Staff.Under Staff, the site's own staff list is a row of names: one tap signs them in.
  2. Type the name, and the company if useful. Sign in.
  3. When they leave, tap Sign out beside them.
More detail

They appear under On site with the time and your name, then under Left today. During a roll call everyone still signed in is listed under Staff, visitors and contractors and marked safe like a resident. Only a kind, a name, an optional company and the times are held, kept as long as the movement log. Signing visitors in and out needs a connection; the roll call keeps the last on-site list on the phone so a drill can still count them.

Admin supervisors and admins

Residents

  1. Add: first name, last name, date of birth.Optionally the ID document, a room and an evacuation need.
  2. Or Import a spreadsheet.Columns: First name, Last name, Date of birth; optionally ID, Building, Floor, Room, Evacuation need. Download the template.
  3. Tap a name to change anything, or mark them departed.Select several then Departed does a whole family at once.
More detail

Import. Every line gets a verdict: ready, already on the register, or the problem to fix. Only the ready lines are added; import again after fixing the sheet and nobody is duplicated. Dates can be day first (14/03/1988) or year first (1988-03-14). The evacuation column can be words, such as "needs help to move" or "wheelchair".

The edit sheet. Details, room, evacuation need, family, authorised absences, breach reports, the rooms they have had, and their history. A departed resident's record stays for the retention period and can be reactivated. Admins can export or erase the whole record from here.

Families. Select several, tick, Family. If one of them is already in a family the others join it. A family stays together on the roll call, children marked, and a room reads as a family.

Authorised absences

  1. Open the resident, find Authorised absence.
  2. First day, last day, a reason from the list.Holiday, family, medical, IPO interview, education, work, other. A holiday is capped at 14 days.
  3. For a child, tick that a parent or guardian agreed.Refused without it.
  • Those days never count as missed
  • Back early: end today cuts it short
  • No destination, no notes are held
Breach reports and the Absences tab

Absences tab. Every active resident with a run of missed nights or a missed day in the rolling window, worst first, each count beside the figure in Settings, with whether they are checked in today or away, and the last breach report. Type a name or room to filter. Nothing is decided here; the letter is the manager's.

Breach reports. When a figure is reached, or a resident misuses the verification tablet by signing in for others, staff issue a breach report to IPAS. Record on the person's sheet that it was issued, which kind, the day and its reference, so nobody issues one twice.

The nightly reminder. With the switch on under Settings, supervisors and admins are emailed the residents at or over a figure after close-out, on the nights there is anyone to list.

Buildings and rooms

  1. Add a building, then its rooms as painted on the doors."1-12" or "G1, G2", with beds per room.
  2. edit a room for beds, contracted beds and bed set-up."double + bunk". The Vacancies report reads these.
  3. archive a room to take it out of use.Its history stays; restore brings it back.
More detail

Assign residents to rooms on their edit sheet. The tab then shows occupancy per room: each person is a chip with a filled dot for on site, a hollow dot for off site, child where under the adult age, and their evacuation need where that feature is on. Tap a chip to open their record. A room never lived in is simply removed; one that was is archived, keeps its history, and takes nobody new.

What each one is

Daily register: one row per resident per day, required, presented, first seen. Attendance: one row per resident with days required, presented and missed. Movement log: every sign in and out. Out and back: one row per absence, out and back on one line, hours away. Absent now, Absent overnight: see off site. Drills and Who was marked safe: the roll-call record. Occupancy and Vacancies: per room, the latter against contracted beds. Evacuation list: everyone with room, presence and need. Administrators also have Who viewed which record.

Staff

  1. Site staff list: paste names, one per line, job title after a comma.Kitchen, cleaning, maintenance. Each becomes a one-tap sign-in on the Visitors tab.
  2. Staff accounts admins: invite by email with a role.They choose their own password from the link. Disable an account when someone leaves.

Settings admins

  • Register rules: adult age, the amber hour, the House Rules figures, the holiday cap
  • Features: buildings, evacuation, households, visitors, the door switch, the nightly reminder
  • Retention: movements and the register
  • Security: idle lock, email codes, home countries
More detail

Features are off until turned on here; turning one off hides its screens and deletes nothing. The IPAS policy says six months for the register; the audit trail and access log follow it. Movements default to 90 days; raise it if you need the log itself for longer, though the overnight snapshot keeps the answer regardless.

Working offline

  • The pill says Offline
  • Everything you record is Queued
  • It is sent, in order, when the connection returns
More detail

In & out and the register keep working when the connection drops; the list you last saw stays on screen. When the connection returns the queue is sent with the time you actually recorded it, and the cards update. A queued event survives a logout and is sent the next time anyone logs in on that device. Events older than the site's sync window are refused and listed so you can re-record them. The copy of the register held on the device is encrypted.

Something is wrong

I am locked out after wrong passwords

Five minutes, then try again. Use Forgot your password? if you are unsure of it.

The screen locked itself

The idle lock. Nothing was lost; log in again.

The code by email did not arrive

Codes last ten minutes. Check spam, then start again from the login screen. If the site cannot send email at all, the message names the security contact: tell your administrator.

"Only an administrator can…" or "Not authorised"

The action is for a higher role. The message names which.

My login was refused because of where I am

Supervisors and admins can only log in from the site's home countries. Ask an admin to add the country under Settings.

A resident is missing from the list

They may be marked departed: under Residents, the Departed filter, then reactivate. Or they were never added.

Something says Queued and stays that way

No connection. The event is safe and will be sent when it returns.

The nightly job is late

A banner appears on the register when close-out has not run by two in the morning. Until then yesterday is not yet on the record as missed. Tell your administrator; it is a hosting matter.

Privacy on one page

  • Held about a resident: name, date of birth, ID document type and number, room, evacuation need, household, authorised absences and breach reports, and every movement, check-in and register day.
  • Who sees what: guards see names, ages and states; the date of birth is for supervisors and admins; the ID number is on the detail sheet only; the evacuation need is on the roll call and evacuation list only.
  • Kept for: movements 90 days, the register six months, both set by the site. The audit trail and the access log follow the register.
  • On the record: every change an administrator makes, every export and its reason, every opening of a resident's record, and every login attempt.
  • A resident's rights: an admin can export everything held about a person as one file, or erase them entirely, leaving only a proof that an erasure happened.
  • Where: one provider, in Frankfurt, in the European Union. Nothing is sent to a third party.

The full statement is in the site's privacy notice and data processing agreement.
